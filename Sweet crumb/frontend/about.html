Born from Passion

Where Flour, Butter, & Memories Meet

Sweet Crumbs was established in the autumn of 2018 by pastry lovers determined to bring true artisan baking back into modern celebrations. Tired of overly sugary, mass-produced supermarket cakes filled with artificial stabilizers, we started baking small batches with authentic Belgian couverture, farm butter, and hand-scraped vanilla beans.

What began as custom orders for family milestones soon blossomed into our studio bakery. Today, our team of dedicated pastry chefs awakens before sunrise every morning to cream butter, temper chocolate, and fold tender layers of puff pastry—ensuring that every bite you take is extraordinarily fresh.

Our Baking Philosophy

Ingredients That Tell a Story

We never take shortcuts. Here is what goes into our mixing bowls every morning.

Pure Vanilla Pods

Real whole Madagascar Bourbon vanilla pods rather than synthetic vanillin flavoring.

Belgian Couverture

55% and 70% pure cocoa-butter chocolate imported from authentic Belgian ateliers.

82% Fat Dairy Butter

High-butterfat European style creamery butter for tender crumb structure and flaky crusts.

Zero Hydrogenated Oils

No margarine, no trans-fats, and no artificial shelf-life extenders. Ever.
